
Privacy Policy for "Sin Notas"

Last Updated: November 2026
At Sin Notas, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled within our desktop and mobile applications.

1. Information Collection and Telemetry

Sin Notas does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or telemetry.
• We do not require you to create an account, or provide an email address or password.
• The application operates completely anonymously.

2. Local Data Storage

All content you create within the application (including notes, journals, flashcards, concept maps, and attached images) is stored exclusively on your own local device.
• Files are saved in local folders as Markdown text and binary cache files under your full control.
• We do not own, maintain, or have access to any servers, databases, or cloud storage services. If you choose to sync your notes across devices, such synchronization relies entirely on the third-party service you independently choose to use.

3. Third-Party Services and Tools

The application does not use third-party tools, SDKs, or services to collect, track, or store your data. We do not include advertisements, behavioral analytics frameworks (such as Google Analytics), or external payment gateways within the software.

4. Data Security

Since the application runs purely locally, the security of your notes depends on the security of your own device and the backup methods you choose to implement. The application's source code undergoes strict sanitization filters to ensure that elements processed on your screen remain secure.

5. Contact and Support

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or the app's functionality, you can reach out directly to the developer through the following channels:
• By clicking the Repository button built into the application's settings.
• By visiting the official GitHub project repository directly at: https://github.com/wulflo/SinNotas/.
